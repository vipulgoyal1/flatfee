San Jose, CA

The capital of Silicon Valley and the Bay Area's largest city

Buying in San Jose

Buying a home in San Jose is a micro-market game: schools, commute paths, and neighborhood lines move pricing more than any citywide average. List prices are marketing, not truth, and the best homes trigger fast multiple-offer situations-especially in Willow Glen, Cambrian, Almaden, Evergreen, and West San Jose.

San Jose demand is powered by tech compensation and stock equity liquidity, while new single-family supply stays structurally constrained. To win here, you move fast, write clean offers, and beat competitors on terms-not just price. Will the prices go up or down? The tech stock index can offer a bigger clue rather than the interest rates.
