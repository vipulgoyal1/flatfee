Fremont, CA

A large East Bay city with elite schools, and strong tech presence

Buying in Fremont

Fremont sits at the pivot point between Silicon Valley and the East Bay-big enough to function like its own metro, yet tight enough that commute corridors and school boundaries dominate buyer decisions. It is the Bay Area's fourth-largest city and home to major employers like the Tesla Factory, which helps keep demand anchored to high-income job growth.

Fremont day-to-day geography is simple: hills to the east (Mission Peak), industry and transit to the west, and neighborhoods wrapped around the 880 and 680 spine plus BART access, including Warm Springs and South Fremont.
