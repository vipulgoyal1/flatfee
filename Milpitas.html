Milpitas, CA

A Santa Clara County city at the north edge of Silicon Valley with strong regional connectivity

Buying in Milpitas

Milpitas is known for its strategic Silicon Valley location, major retail anchors like the Great Mall, and strong access to both freeways and BART. It has become an increasingly practical choice for buyers who want convenience across the South Bay.

The city includes a mix of established neighborhoods and newer developments, each with different tradeoffs in commute, density, and home style. Strong results usually come from a clear priority list before touring begins.
