San Mateo, CA

A Peninsula stronghold with elite neighborhoods and fast access to SF + Silicon Valley

Buying in San Mateo

San Mateo County is home to a significant fraction of tech CEOs and leaders, and a home here is a prized asset. The city blends a vibrant downtown with established residential pockets that feel very different block to block.

Prestigious neighborhoods like Baywood-Aragon are known for tree-lined streets and strong schools, while areas near Bay Meadows and Caltrain offer a more transit-oriented lifestyle. That mix creates distinct submarkets, each with its own pace and buyer profile.
