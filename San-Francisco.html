San Francisco, CA

    A beautiful city that is the cultural and financial capital of Northern California

Buying a residential home in San Francisco requires dealing with many unique aspects such as the lottery system for schools and a famously opaque home pricing culture. List prices mean very little and having an experienced buyer's agent is important to help win the bidding wars.

Multiple upcoming AI IPOs for companies like OpenAI and Anthropic promise to unlock significant home purchasing power in the next couple of years. With very little new inventory, this means that buying a home in the city is only going to get more competitive. 




SF Real Estate Trends


While spring and summer of 2025 were a balanced market giving an opportunity to the buyers, this changed in the Fall. San Francisco ended 2025 with rising prices and tighter inventory—conditions that typically indicate a return to seller's market.
