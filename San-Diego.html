San Diego, CA

A sun-soaked coastal powerhouse that anchors Southern California's tech, military, and biotech

Buying in San Diego

Set on the Pacific coast beside the Mexico border, San Diego is a city of mesas, canyons, and a working bay. That geography creates hard neighborhood edges-La Jolla, North Park, and Point Loma behave like separate markets, and pricing can swing fast across short distances.

The city blends military, biotech, universities, and cross-border commerce, which gives San Diego demand depth across multiple industries. Coastal enclaves, central urban neighborhoods, and inland communities each attract different buyer priorities and lifestyles.
